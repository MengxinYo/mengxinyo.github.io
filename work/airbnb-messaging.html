Overview

Messaging is the connective tissue of Airbnb — the one surface every guest and host passes through, regardless of which product line brought them there. As Airbnb expanded beyond stays into Experiences and Services, that single-purpose inbox needed to become a flexible foundation for entirely new kinds of conversations.

I co-led the end-to-end design of that rebuild: from the core message framework to the features layered on top, culminating in a flagship launch for the 2024 Airbnb Summer Release.

/ The Challenge

One inbox, many futures

A conversation model built for one use case had to support several — without breaking the simple, trusted thread guests and hosts already relied on.

  • The existing model was built around a single scenario: a guest messaging a host about a stay. It had no concept of group planning, service coordination, or time-sensitive logistics.
  • Trust and safety tooling — built by other teams on top of the old architecture — could not regress during the rebuild.
  • Any new structure had to scale across every future business line, not just the two on the immediate roadmap.

/ The Approach

A flexible core, not a bigger inbox

Instead of adding one-off screens per business line, we rebuilt Messaging around a small set of primitives every future product could compose from.

/ A Closer Look

Designing the message framework

The hardest part wasn't any single screen — it was agreeing on the handful of primitives everything else would be built from. We spent the early months mapping every message type Messaging would ever need to render, then collapsing that list into a small component library any team could compose without touching the underlying thread logic.

New content types became a composition problem instead of a rebuild — which is what let Experiences and Services ship on the same foundation later without a second migration.
